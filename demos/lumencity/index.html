        Mini Project · CB-334

        Smart City Light Management System

        A street-lighting platform that switches, dims and monitors every light in a city based on daylight, weather, traffic and time. It cuts energy use while keeping roads safe.

        Student
        Sadaphule Prathmesh Rahuldeo

        RBT24CB034
        Roll No.

        Problem

        Conventional street lights run at full power from dusk to dawn, even on empty streets. Failed lamps go unnoticed until someone complains, and nobody can see or control the network as a whole.

        Objectives

        • Switch lights automatically using ambient light (LDR)
        • Dim empty streets and brighten on motion (PIR sensor)
        • Adapt to weather such as rain and fog
        • Detect faults and dispatch maintenance crews
        • Measure energy, cost and CO₂ savings

        System architecture

        Smart light nodeLED, LDR, PIR sensor, ESP32
        Zone gatewayLoRa or Wi-Fi mesh
        Control centrePolicies, schedules, analytics
        MaintenanceAlerts and crew dispatch

        Control logic

        1. Daylight. If ambient light is above the threshold, the light stays off.
        2. Base level. The zone policy or active schedule sets the dimmed level.
        3. Weather. Rain or fog adds 20% for visibility.
        4. Motion. A vehicle or person in range raises the light to full.
        5. Fade. The level ramps smoothly, with no flicker.
