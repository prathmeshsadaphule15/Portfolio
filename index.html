02 / 06Smart city · IoT

LumenCity

A control centre for 104 smart street lights that dim, brighten and report their own faults.

Each light reacts to daylight, motion, weather and schedules. Operators track energy and CO₂ saved and send repair vans along the shortest route. No libraries, plus an ESP32 sketch for real hardware.

(03) About

Curious about why a model works, not just whether.

I'm Prathmesh, from Solapur, now studying in Pune. I enjoy the part of a project most people skip: checking the numbers, explaining the result, and making the interface clear enough that someone else can use it.

In ClickForest I wrote a Random Forest from scratch in NumPy and checked it against scikit-learn: 99.2% of predictions agree. When Logistic Regression beat my forest, I ran a significance test and reported it instead of hiding the result.

I'm looking for an internship in machine learning, data or web development, where I can learn from people who ship real products and contribute from the first week.
